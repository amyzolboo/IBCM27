title: Mongolia to host GB34, the 34th GBIF Governing Board meeting, in 2027
lang: en
ref: gb34-mongolia
date: 2026-11-06 18:00:00 +0800
---

Mongolia has been selected to host **GB34**, the 34th meeting of the Governing Board of the
[Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF)](https://www.gbif.org/what-is-gbif), in 2027.

GB34 will be held together with **IBCM27**, the International Biological Conference in Mongolia,
organized by the Institute of Biology, Mongolian Academy of Sciences. Bringing the two events together
will connect Mongolian researchers with the global GBIF community and showcase Mongolia's rich biodiversity
to an international audience.

GBIF Mongolia, hosted by the Institute of Biology, supports the mobilization, publication and use of
biodiversity data from Mongolia. More details about GB34 and joint activities will be announced as
preparations progress.
